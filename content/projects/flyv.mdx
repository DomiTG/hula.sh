# Flyv

> A complete rethink of everything I learned while building Avionify.

---

## Why Flyv exists

Flyv wasn't created because I wanted a different name.

It exists because I wanted to build the platform I originally imagined, without carrying years of architectural compromises.

After developing Avionify, I reached a point where I spent more time working around previous decisions than building new ideas. Rather than continuously patching the project, I archived it and started again with a clear goal: **design the platform around long-term scalability instead of short-term convenience.**

---

## More than a website

At its core, Flyv is a platform for the flight simulation community.

Pilots can build their careers, join virtual airlines, manage fleets, complete contracts, earn achievements and interact with a persistent ecosystem designed to connect every part of the experience.

Instead of treating these features as separate applications, they are designed to work together as one system.

That philosophy influences every technical decision behind the project.

## Building the foundation

One of the biggest differences compared to previous projects is the amount of time invested in the foundation before adding features.

Authentication is designed as a standalone identity service.

Permissions are role-based and reusable across the entire platform.

Media uploads are handled through dedicated storage services.

Real-time notifications are delivered through Server-Sent Events.

Database models are structured to support future expansion rather than only today's requirements.

Much of the development isn't immediately visible to users, but it makes adding new functionality significantly easier.

---

## Technology

Flyv is built almost entirely with TypeScript.

The frontend uses **Next.js**, **React** and **Tailwind CSS**, while the backend relies on **tRPC**, **Prisma**, **MySQL** and **Redis**. File storage is handled through **AWS S3**, with Docker used for local development and deployment.

The project follows a component-driven approach with a strong focus on type safety, reusable abstractions and maintainable code.

---

## Current focus

Flyv is still under active development.

The current work focuses on expanding the airline management experience, improving the pilot career system and refining the overall user experience before the platform becomes publicly available.

Rather than shipping features as quickly as possible, I prefer building systems that can continue to evolve without requiring major rewrites.

---

## What this project means to me

Flyv is the largest software project I've worked on.

It combines everything I've learned from previous projects into a single codebase—from frontend development and backend architecture to authentication, infrastructure, databases and real-time communication.

More importantly, it represents a different way of approaching software development.

Instead of asking **"How can I build this feature?"**, I now spend much more time asking **"How will this decision affect the next hundred features?"**

That shift in mindset has probably had a bigger impact on the project than any individual technology.
