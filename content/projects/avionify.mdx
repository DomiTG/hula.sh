## **Introduction**
Avionify is a passion-driven project spearheaded by me, **Dominik**, after years of enjoying and exploring flight simulators. I aimed to create something new and valuable for the flight simulation community, offering a rich and engaging experience.

Originally conceived as a pilot-focused application called *Aviationify*, the initial attempt fell short. However, it provided valuable experience and led to the formation of a collaborative effort with another developer. Together, we reimagined the concept, leading to the birth of *Avionify*.

With Avionify, we offer users the ability to engage as both pilots and managers, managing airlines and connecting with others in the community for a deeply immersive experience.

## **What is Avionify?**
**Avionify** transforms flight simulators by adding dynamic career modes. Whether you’ve dreamed of managing your own airline or excelling as a pilot, Avionify provides the tools to fulfill your ambitions.

### **Pilot Career Mode**
- Start as a rookie pilot and work your way up by completing flights, earning promotions, and gaining in-game currency.
- Maintain a strong safety record—no crashes allowed!
- As you progress, licence yourself for new aircraft and explore a variety of routes.

### **Airline Manager Mode**
- Reach **Level 10** as a pilot to unlock the airline management mode.
- Use your app currency to establish and grow your company.
- Hire other Avionify users as pilots, build a successful fleet, and dominate the skies.

This dual-mode approach adds depth, strategy, and engagement to flight simulation, making Avionify a one-of-a-kind tool for aviation enthusiasts.

## **Key Features**
- **Immersive Career Progression**: Level up as a pilot with realistic challenges.
- **Dynamic Airline Management**: Design your fleet, recruit employees, and manage operations.
- **Community-Driven Gameplay**: Interact with other users for hiring, collaboration, and competitive rankings.
- **Simulator Integration**: Seamless connection with major flight simulators using **FSUIPC**, with plans for a proprietary connector in the future.

## **Technology Stack**
### **Backend**
- **TypeScript** for consistent, maintainable code.
- **Express** framework for API routes and efficient server-side logic.
- **WebSocket** for real-time communication between app and server.
- **MySQL** for robust and scalable data management.

### **Frontend**
- **Electron** for a cross-platform desktop application experience.
- **React** for dynamic, responsive interfaces.
- **TailwindCSS** for modern and flexible styling.

## **Planned Features**
- **Custom Simulator Connector**: Replace FSUIPC with a native solution for enhanced performance.
- **Advanced Airline Customization**: Introduce detailed branding options, route planning tools, and analytics for managers.
- **Global Leaderboards**: Showcase top pilots and airlines based on performance metrics.
- **Event Integration**: Host competitive airline challenges and live events.

## **Conclusion**
Avionify is more than just an addon; it’s a platform for aviation enthusiasts to explore new horizons. By combining technical excellence with an engaging career structure, we aim to redefine the flight simulation experience. Join us as we take flight into an exciting future!
